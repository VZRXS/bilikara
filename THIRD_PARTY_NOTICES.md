## QR image generation

Internet Remote and login QR PNGs are generated by `bilikara_runtime` using
[`qrcode` 0.14.1](https://github.com/kennytm/qrcode-rust) and
[`png` 0.18.1](https://github.com/image-rs/image-png), both MIT OR Apache-2.0.
Both dependencies disable default features. P03 reuses the same QR encoder for
playlist/history footers. Python `qrcode` and Pillow are no longer production
dependencies; Pillow remains an independent test decoder and preview-script tool.

Playlist/history rendering uses [COSMIC Text 0.17](https://github.com/pop-os/cosmic-text)
(MIT OR Apache-2.0), including fontdb, HarfRust and Swash for font discovery,
shaping, variable font weights and rasterization. The bundled Source Han Sans
font remains primary; installed system fonts supply fallback without downloads.
[chrono](https://github.com/chronotope/chrono) (MIT OR Apache-2.0) formats local
time; [csv](https://github.com/BurntSushi/rust-csv) (MIT OR Unlicense) writes CSV.
The existing `png` and `zip` crates encode sequential image pages and archives.
The independent `zxing-cpp` decoder in `requirements-test-qr.txt` is test-only
(Apache-2.0) and is not included in packaging requirements.
